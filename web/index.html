Three-Body Gravity Engine

A live simulation of Newton's gravity. Watch stars and planets pull on each other, explore famous three-body orbits, and see why the three-body problem is chaotic: move one body by a billionth, and eventually everything turns out differently.

Try one

How to use

Preset
Pick a starting setup: classic orbits (figure-8, Lagrange triangle, Euler line), a Trojan asteroid, a moon around a planet, or the chaotic Pythagorean problem.
Play, Reset, Speed
Pause and resume (Space), start over, or run from 0.1× to 10× speed.
Trail length
How much of each body's path stays visible behind it.
Camera
Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan, or jump to a tilted, top or side view.
Chaos comparison
Run nudged copies next to the original: a ghost twin, a split screen, or a cloud of 30. The chart shows how fast the gap grows. A straight line on it means chaos.
Readout
Simulation time, and the energy error: real energy never changes, so this shows how accurate the simulation is (smaller is better).
Hide
Press H to hide the controls and fill the screen. Press ? in the panel to see this page again.
